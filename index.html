Sumana Saman's Invitation

Chapter 1: The Divine Request

God Sumana Saman, the guardian deity of Samanala Kanda (Adam's Peak), visits Lord Buddha during His visit to Kelaniya. Bowing with deep devotion, the deity invites Lord Buddha to climb the holy peak of Samanala Mountain and place His sacred footprint on the summit, establishing a place of worship for all generations.
